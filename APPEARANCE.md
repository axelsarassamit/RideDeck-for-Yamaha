# RideDeck family appearance

Appearance is independent of the accent theme. System (the default) follows Android; Light and Dark override Android. Lime, Ice blue, Amber, White and the edition blue each have light and dark colours with readable foreground contrast. White uses neutral slate ink in light mode.

Dash defaults to Follow RideDeck. Each resumed, authenticated Standard or Yamaha app sends its saved mode and accent through the existing verified provider. Dash remembers the last received choice across process restarts. System is sent as a mode, so Dash continues to follow Android without needing the host running. Older/absent Dash does not prevent the host opening. Appearance calls cannot create or renew a recording session.

Dash Appearance offers Follow RideDeck, System, Light and Dark. Selecting a standalone mode or accent leaves Follow RideDeck until it is selected again. The last received host choice is retained while overridden. The two host apps retain their own saved settings and edition branding; Dash follows the most recently opened one.

Theme changes rebuild app screens and native dialogs. Recording services, stored clips, camera/export overlays and Yamaha bike-display day/night rules are independent of app appearance. Standard continues to use external map apps.
