# RideDeck family controls

The three editions share light/dark surfaces, 8 dp corners, readable accent colours and labelled primary controls. System, Light and Dark plus accent choice live together in Appearance, with a real-colour preview. Wide screens use a two-column appearance page.

Standard and Yamaha use the same action order: their map/bike action, Camera, Voice, ride timer, Dash. Right-side placement mirrors the whole rail in both hosts. Setup remains in the header, or the compact action rail. Each action retains its edition-specific function. Standard uses external maps.

Camera recording status combines words and symbols. Fresh recording is visually distinct from connecting, stopped and unavailable; stale replies cannot claim recording. Error/recording red adapts to light and dark surfaces. Dash exposes Setup and Recordings directly and uses the same line-icon language and large targets. Recording and power policies are unchanged.

Yamaha short landscape music gives metadata its own row above the playback controls. Long metadata is bounded and does not move the controls. Main controls retain at least 56 dp target height, with 64 dp normal action tiles.

Brand artwork uses the original road-shaped R with rounded strokes, tight bounds and a transparent canvas. Header marks are large enough to read without a square tile. Dash speed, GPS and stabilization readouts remain transparent over the camera view. GX12 is not a visible product name; legacy Android identifiers remain compatible with installed apps.
