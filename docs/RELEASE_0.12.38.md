# Yamaha 0.12.38

Navigation now gives advance, approach and immediate turn prompts, rather than one announcement inside 200 metres. Timing adapts to current GPS speed: advance warnings are approximately 30 seconds ahead (300 to 1,000 metres), approach reminders 12 seconds ahead (100 to 400 metres), and immediate instructions 3 seconds ahead (20 to 80 metres).

Starting navigation gives the first instruction. Long uninterrupted stretches receive a reminder after at least three minutes and one kilometre of forward progress. Closely spaced turns include a short next-turn preview when within 120 metres. Arrival, confirmed off-route recalculation and loss of a previously acquired GPS signal are announced once per event. GPS jumps do not replay already spoken stages; late fixes skip obsolete warnings. Muting navigation still disables all prompts, and the existing English-only voice selection remains in place.

TomTom routes also announce reported traffic delay of at least two minutes, and meaningful increases of at least two minutes. Warnings wait until direction guidance has finished and there is room before the next turn. Stable delay is not repeated; delay information older than ten minutes is not spoken. Existing five-minute traffic route refresh can supply new delay information when enabled.

Accident and road-closure reports are checked in the next 10 km of the route, initially and every five minutes. Only current certain/probable reports with valid geometry are considered. A 15-metre corridor and line-alignment checks reject reports behind the rider, different roads and crossing streets where the geometry permits it. This remains proximity matching: it cannot establish lane, direction or elevation. Voice and graphic banners explicitly say the event is reported near the route. Accident and closure symbols appear on both map displays without covering turn instructions, the speed sign, current-road strip or attribution. Each incident gets at most one advance and one close reminder per route session, with a 30-second gap between alerts. Reports older than six minutes disappear. A nearby reported closure can request a new traffic-aware TomTom route check; actual avoidance depends on that provider's route result. Turning off navigation voice keeps the graphic alert.

The alert switch is in Map and routing accounts. This feature adds Traffic API requests, normally at most one every five minutes, with a one-minute minimum on route changes. TomTom key/access, internet, coverage and usage allowances apply. Failed requests never mean the road is clear. No raw provider reports, API keys or rider positions are added to the repository.

Automatic traffic refresh does not repeat the route-start announcement. Bike authentication/data and Dash recording behavior are unchanged. Acceptance requires scheduler/speech unit tests, encoding/build checks and verification of the installed build. Actual announcement timing and audibility on a ride still require a physical route test.
