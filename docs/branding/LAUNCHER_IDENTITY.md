# Approved launcher identity, 2026-10-21

Owner-approved Canva concept: https://www.canva.com/M/MAHYqHnUnO8

Standard uses a blue field and white Roadline R. Yamaha uses a navy R field with a separate white Yamaha Motor panel. Dash uses an orange field with an R and camera silhouette. Colour and silhouette distinguish the three apps at phone launcher sizes.

Android adaptive backgrounds and foregrounds remain separate. The foreground artwork uses the 108-unit adaptive canvas with the essential marks centred in the safe region. Legacy launcher rendering has the matching background baked in. Source geometry is in launcher-mark.svg. PNG exports are antialiased at 1080 pixels.

For Yamaha, the red tuning-fork emblem and wordmark come from the existing original yamaha_motor_logo.png, cropped to its upper 1042 x 224 logo region without stretching, recolouring or redrawing. The supplied manual is at https://branding.yamaha-motor.co.th/en/branding-edutainment/how-to-use-yamaha-logo/ . This records source provenance and the owner-approved layout, not a claim of Yamaha endorsement or approval. The original wordmark export is retained alongside the SVG.

Yamaha's in-app header and startup use a separate full-proportion Yamaha panel beneath the RideDeck R. Light mode changes RideDeck ink only. The Yamaha control emblem retains its original red rather than inheriting theme accent colours. Existing app functions, identifiers, certificate checks and signers are preserved.
